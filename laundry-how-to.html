## Sports clothes Washing your cycling clothes properly helps preserve their functionality, comfort, and longevity. Here’s a step-by-step guide: --- ### 🧼 **Before Washing** 1. **Turn garments inside out**: This helps protect the fabric and graphics from wear during washing. 2. **Close zippers and fasteners**: Prevents snagging on other items. 3. **Pre-rinse if needed**: If your gear is particularly sweaty or muddy, a quick cold-water rinse helps remove excess grime and salts. --- ### 🚿 **Washing Instructions** * **Use cold water (30°C / 86°F or less)**: High heat can damage technical fabrics and elastic components. * **Use a gentle cycle**: Avoid rough agitation to maintain fabric structure. * **Mild detergent only**: * Avoid bleach, fabric softener, or strong stain removers—they can degrade performance fabrics and water-wicking properties. * Use a sportswear-specific detergent if possible. * **Wash similar items together**: Only wash cycling clothes with other synthetic activewear, not with heavy items like jeans or towels. --- ### ☀️ **Drying Your Gear** * **Air-dry only**: Lay flat or hang to dry away from direct sunlight. * **Avoid the dryer**: Heat can damage elastic materials and degrade performance fabrics over time. --- ### ✅ **Additional Tips** * **Wash soon after use**: Sweat and bacteria break down fabric if left too long. * **Use a laundry bag**: Helps protect delicate items like bib straps and mesh panels. * **Don’t iron or dry clean**: High heat and chemicals can ruin technical fabrics. --- Let me know if you want specific care instructions for items like chamois shorts, rain gear, or merino wool jerseys—they each have slightly different needs.
