How to Wash Cycling Clothes

🧼 Before Washing

  • Turn garments inside out: Protects fabric and graphics from wear.
  • Close zippers and fasteners: Prevents snagging.
  • Pre-rinse if needed: Removes grime and salts from sweaty/muddy gear.

🚿 Washing Instructions

  • Use cold water: Max 30°C / 86°F to protect fabrics.
  • Use a gentle cycle: Avoid rough agitation.
  • Mild detergent only: No bleach, softener, or harsh chemicals. Sportswear-specific detergent is best.
  • Wash similar items together: Keep with synthetics, not heavy fabrics like jeans.

☀️ Drying Your Gear

  • Air-dry only: Hang or lay flat away from direct sunlight.
  • Avoid the dryer: Heat damages elastic and performance materials.

✅ Additional Tips

  • Wash soon after use: Prevents bacteria and sweat damage.
  • Use a laundry bag: Protects straps and mesh.
  • Don’t iron or dry clean: Can ruin technical fabrics.
